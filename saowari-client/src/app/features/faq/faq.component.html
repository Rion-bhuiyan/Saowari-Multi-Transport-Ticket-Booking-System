Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries regarding reservations, cancellations, and transport policies.

01 What is Saowari?

Saowari is a centralized, multi-modal transport ticket booking system. It handles real-time seat, berth, and cabin mapping across three transport networks: intercity buses, river launch liners, and domestic flights, eliminating manual counter travel entirely.

02 What modes of transport can I book through Saowari?

You can book tickets for Bus services (AC/Non-AC), Launch/Vessel services (Cabin/Deck), and Airplane services (Domestic/International flights) all from a single unified platform.

03 How do I book tickets for multiple transport types?

From the main search form on the homepage, select your preferred transport type (Bus, Launch, or Flight), pick your origin and destination, input travel dates, and click search. The system will display all real-time departures, letting you visualize seat maps and purchase instantly.

04 Can I choose my preferred seats or cabins?

Yes! Saowari features an interactive visual layout. The seat map matches the physical vehicle configuration: 2+2 or 2+3 seat numbers for buses; VIP, 1st Class, or 2nd Class deck cabins for launches; and economy or business seating schemes for airplane models. Labeled seats display color indicators: green (available), blue (your selection), and red (already booked).

05 Is there any risk of double-booking my seat?

No. Saowari uses a real-time database locking mechanism. The moment you select a seat and proceed to payment, that seat is temporarily locked for up to 10 minutes, guaranteeing no one else can book it simultaneously.

06 What payment methods are supported?

We support secure multi-gateway payments including mobile banking (bKash, Nagad, Rocket), as well as local and international Credit/Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard). We also support direct bank transfers for corporate bookings.

07 Are there any hidden charges or agent fees?

Absolutely not. Saowari guarantees company-direct pricing. There are no third-party agent markups, no informal fees, and no price manipulation. You pay the exact official fare plus standard, transparent taxes.

08 How do I receive my ticket after payment?

Immediately after a successful payment, the system generates a professional PDF e-ticket embedded with a unique QR code. It is instantly available to download from your dashboard and also emailed to your registered email address.

09 Do I need to print my e-ticket to board?

No printed copy is necessary! You can simply open the PDF on your smartphone and show the QR code. Boarding staff will scan the screen directly to verify your ticket authenticity.

10 What is the cancellation and refund policy?

We operate a transparent, tiered auto-refund cancellation structure. For buses and launches: 72+ hours before departure provides a 100% full refund; 24 to 72 hours provides a 50% refund. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before departure are non-refundable. (Flight policies may vary, retaining a 20% processing fee for 72+ hours).

11 How long does it take to get my refund?

Once you confirm a cancellation from your dashboard, the system calculates the refund amount in real-time. Approved refunds are automatically processed back to your original payment method (bKash, card, bank) within the configured processing window.

12 Can I reschedule my journey instead of cancelling?

Yes, subject to seat availability, customers may reschedule a booking to a different date or time. Any fare difference is collected or refunded during the rescheduling process in real-time.

13 Does Saowari provide trip reminders?

Yes. Our automated notification system sends SMS and email reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before your scheduled departure, including full trip details, so you never miss your journey.

14 Can I save passenger details for faster future bookings?

Yes. Frequent travellers can save passenger details (like family members) in their profile dashboard. These saved profiles can be pre-filled during checkout, making multi-passenger bookings faster and error-free.

15 Can I get an invoice for my business travel expenses?

Yes. Full tax invoices for each completed booking are automatically generated in PDF format and are permanently accessible from the 'My Bookings' section of your account dashboard.

16 Does Saowari support promo codes and discounts?

Yes. Customers can enter promo/coupon codes at checkout to receive applicable discounts on base fares. Discounts are validated in real-time against current promotional campaigns.

17 What if I lose my internet connection at the boarding gate?

Since your e-ticket is sent via email and can be downloaded as a PDF immediately after booking, you can save it directly to your device storage. It will remain accessible offline at the boarding gate.

18 Can I still buy tickets at a physical counter?

Yes. While Saowari offers a 24/7 online platform, authorized counter staff also use the same system. You can walk into a physical counter, and an agent can book a ticket for you and print a physical receipt, ensuring a unified inventory.
