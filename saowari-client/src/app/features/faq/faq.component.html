Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries regarding reservations, cancellations, and transport policies.

01 What is Saowari?

Saowari is a centralized, multi-modal transport ticket booking system. It handles real-time seat, berth, and cabin mapping across three transport networks: intercity buses, river launch liners, and domestic flights, eliminating manual counter travel entirely.

02 How do I book tickets for multiple transport types?

From the main search form on the homepage, select your preferred transport type (Bus, Launch, or Flight), pick your origin and destination cities, input travel dates, and click search. The system will display all real-time departures, letting you visualize seat maps and purchase instantly.

03 Can I choose my preferred seats or cabins?

Yes! Saowari is integrated with dynamic visual layouts. The seat map matches the physical vessel configuration: 2+2 or 2+3 seat numbers for buses; VIP, 1st Class, or 2nd Class deck cabins for launches; and economy or business seating schemes for airplane models. Labeled seats display color indicators: green (available), blue (your selection), and red (already booked).

04 What is the cancellation and refund policy?

We operate a transparent, tiered auto-refund cancellation structure:

• 72+ hours before departure: 100% full refund returned to the original payment source.
• 24 to 72 hours before departure: 50% partial refund automatically calculated.
• Less than 24 hours: Non-refundable as inventory stands locked for operational departure.

05 What happens after completing a payment?

Immediately upon transaction verification, the platform generates a digital PDF e-ticket complete with traveler details, cabin numbers, date, and a secure QR code. The QR code contains a cryptographically signed Booking Reference Number that counter agents can scan at travel gates.

06 How secure is the platform?

Saowari takes data safety seriously. All passwords are hashed using high-level BCrypt schemas, REST API endpoints are protected using secure JWT authorization headers, and customer checkout sessions utilize secure SSL/payment callback webhooks to prevent fraud.
