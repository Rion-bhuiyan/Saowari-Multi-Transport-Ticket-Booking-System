Compliance & Safety

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

At Saowari, we take the confidentiality of your personal and transaction data seriously. All sensitive passenger datasets processed during account creation, ticket checkout, or profile dashboard updates are safeguarded within highly secured infrastructures.

1. Information We Collect

We collect full names, email addresses, verified mobile phone numbers, and profile photos. During checkout, secure transaction tokens are parsed from payment gateways; card or credential values are never handled nor stored by our servers.

2. Security Compliance Measures

All client passwords are automatically hashed using the industry-standard BCrypt database framework. Web API queries are guarded via secure, time-expiring JWT Authorization Tokens to prevent session hijack or unauthorized backend calls.

3. Contact for Data Access

Customers can permanently update or delete personal data, saved family profiles, and notification preferences at any point from their personal dashboards, or request immediate data deletion by contacting support@saowari.com.

Operational Terms

Terms of Service

Last updated: April 2026

By registering an account on Saowari or completing any passenger ticket reservation, you explicitly agree to follow the standard operating guidelines and passenger terms defined herein.

1. Centralized Real-Time Inventory

The ticket search, seat maps, and cabin allocations are fully dynamic. Selectable inventory is tracked directly in a centralized relational database. Once a seat is selected, a short-lived transaction lock is applied. If payment fails to authorize before the lock expires, the seats are instantly returned to the public pool.

2. E-Ticket & QR Authentication

Successful payments generate a digital PDF e-ticket containing a unique, cryptographically signed QR code. The QR code represents your valid travel pass. Counter staff and boarding agents at departure terminals will scan the code to verify authenticity.

3. Boarding & Gate Restrictions

Passengers are requested to arrive at boarding docks, launch terminals, or airport check-in counters at least 30 minutes prior to scheduled departures. Failure to present a valid QR e-ticket or matching ID may result in immediate gate denial.

Refund Terms

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Cancellations on Saowari follow a structured, policy-based automated refund model. Customers can submit cancellation requests for upcoming trips directly from their user dashboards within the policy timeline.

Tiered Auto-Refund Rates

72+ Hours Before 100% Refund Zero Deductions
24 to 72 Hours 50% Refund Half Fare Retained
Under 24 Hours 0% Refund Non-Refundable

Processing Window

Once an auto-refund is successfully confirmed, the transaction is routed back to your original payment method (bKash, credit/debit card, or bank transfer). Crediting typically completes within 3 to 7 working days based on your bank's operational policies.
