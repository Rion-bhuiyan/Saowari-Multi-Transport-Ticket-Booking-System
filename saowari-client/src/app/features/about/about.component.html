About Saowari

Your Trusted Intelligent Multi-Modal Transport Booking Platform

Our Mission

Revolutionizing Travel Management

Saowari is a centralized, intelligent, and fully automated online ticket booking and management platform developed specifically for a privately owned multi-modal transportation company. By consolidating Bus, Launch (waterway vessels), and Airplane services under a single unified platform, we empower travellers to seamlessly search, select, book, and pay for tickets from any device at any time.

Our primary objective is to modernize transportation services by replacing manual, counter-based ticketing with a robust digital infrastructure. This reduces human error, eliminates double-bookings, improves inventory distribution, and delivers premium, real-time business intelligence for unified transport management.

Centralized & Seamless

One account. Three transport modes. Zero hassle.

Transport Services Covered

Bus Services

Access company-owned intercity and local bus routes. Experience real-time seat availability maps (e.g. 2+2, 2+3 layouts), precise booking control, and transparent fare structures across all major regional destinations.

Launch / Waterway

Book waterway passenger vessels operating across major river routes. Effortlessly choose between 1st Class, 2nd Class, and luxurious VIP deck cabins with smart capacity allocation and accurate departure coordinates.

Airplane Services

Book domestic and international flight tickets directly. View real-time airline flight calendars, select preferred cabin classes (Economy/Business), and instantly download secure, high-quality digital e-tickets.

Our Core Project Objectives

1
Unified Booking Experience

A complete end-to-end online journey from quick route searches to checkout and digital ticket issuance.

2
Zero Double-Booking

State-of-the-art transactional locking prevents simultaneous ticket allocation on our centralized inventory.

3
Secure Multi-Gateway Payments

Integrated with mobile banking networks (bKash, Nagad) alongside secure bank transfers for maximum flexibility.

4
Cost Reduction & Modernization

Modern relational data models replace manual paper ledgers with robust SQL Server tracking.
